QUICK HELP
  • Knobs: drag up/down. Hold Shift for fine control. Double-click to reset. Mouse wheel works too.
  • A / B: A plays your original mix (bypassed), B plays the master. Level-matched A/B comes later.
  • Loudness: turn up Drive until Integrated reaches your target. True Peak stays at or below the Ceiling.
  • Soft Clipper: turn it on, set Threshold so it shaves 1–3 dB, then raise Drive on the Maximizer for a louder, punchier master.
  • Learn: the right panel explains what each control is doing to your track, live.
